## 👋 About me

**Full-Stack Developer Intern at Nexvia Technologies** · Bengaluru

I build **SaaS platforms, marketplaces and AI-powered apps** with TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js and PostgreSQL, and I take them from database design to deployment. At Nexvia I've shipped two live platforms: **TaskFlow**, a multi-tenant project management SaaS, and **ShopSphere**, a multi-vendor marketplace, with **513 automated tests** between them. Outside work I'm building **PaperWeave**, an AI research assistant; earlier projects include **Groundwork**, a PDF assistant that cites its pages, and **Aizen Map**, live on Netlify.

My path into development runs through two years of **tech support and IT recruitment**. Working with users and hiring teams taught me to turn vague requests into clear requirements, stay calm under pressure, and explain technical work to anyone — and I bring that to every feature I ship.

- 🎯 **Focus** — SaaS platforms · marketplaces · AI-powered apps
